Trauma Surgery

24x7 trauma and fracture care, including facial bone fractures, at Manuel Medical Centre.

The casualty is open day and night, with doctors available around the clock. Trauma surgery covers fractures and facio-maxillary injuries — fractures of the facial bones. A free ambulance can be requested for enquiry and referral.

What this service covers

  • 24x7 trauma care
  • Fracture treatment
  • Facio-maxillary and facial bone fractures
  • Casualty — 6 beds
  • Emergency ICU — 4 beds
  • Free ambulance — 94435 92526
